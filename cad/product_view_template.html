    How the stage drives it

    • BugSorter moves one stage between its top and bottom limit switches at constant speed. Its default stroke is 2 cm.
    • Rod A hangs from that stage at x = +R and pins levels 1 and 3. Rod B at x = −R pins levels 2 and 4. When the stage rises, levels on rod A lift their right ends and levels on rod B lift their left ends, so neighbours tilt opposite ways.
    • Each rod's cross-pin rides a short slot in its level's drive lug. The rod stays vertical and the slot turns with the box, so the tilt is θ = atan(stroke / 2R).
    R = 56.7 mm · 2.0 cm stroke → ±10.0°

    At 55 mm spacing, neighbouring boxes touch at a 2.5 cm stroke (±12.6°). Keep the stroke at or under 2.3 cm in the web app. Wider angles need the levels hung further apart.

    Geometry from cad/product.py in the testSieveSorter repo. The holder box is rebuilt from a drawing, not measured, and the linkage hasn't been built. Sim results that guided the parts are uncalibrated.
